THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md lists the compiled dependencies, the code whose behavior phit follows, and their licenses, with the license texts in LICENSES/. Release archives include both.

This does not relicense third-party material:

  • Bundled published datasets retain their original provenance and licenses. See Gaussian mixed-model data, the preserved package license, dataset provenance, and cbpp provenance.
  • Rust dependencies retain the licenses declared in their own packages. The explicit sampling dependencies rand 0.9.5 and rand_chacha 0.9.0 use the MIT option of their dual licenses; notices and source hashes are retained under third-party/ and third-party/sources.json.
  • Vendored Quarto web assets retain their embedded third-party notices.
  • R, ggplot2 and other development tools retain their own licenses; they are not runtime dependencies of the PHP extension.

The MIT choice covers project-owned files; preserve applicable third-party notices when redistributing the library, documentation or data.
